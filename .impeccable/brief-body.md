# Surface brief — Landing (app/page.tsx)

Scope and visitor mode: whole-site redirection exercise; landing is a **Persuade** surface, the storefront that must make the offer intelligible in the opening viewport.

Audience: competitive gamers who pay for rank climbs and in-game services — they want results, trust, and zero friction, not bargain pricing.

Job: prove the catalog is real and run the funnel (browse store → configure → checkout). Primary action: "View the ladder" → /store.

Proof/content: live catalog from the database (games + per-game service counts), honest how-it-works, transparency section, no invented claims.

Constraints: code-led build (no image generation), English copy, Rowmodz brand confirmed, dark violet palette, one orchestrated motion moment, contrast >= 4.5:1, the old lime/BOOST-PRO world is replaced, not polished.

Chosen direction: **Ranked Ladder** — the product IS the standings sheet; the catalog renders as a season leaderboard. Memorable moment: the standings rows rising on load, the top row drawn with the violet rank rule.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product climbs ranks, so the page IS a standings table — the catalog is a live leaderboard, not a storefront hero. It refuses the boost-shop default of a neon hero, a "book your booster" card, and a grid of clipart game tiles.

OWN-WORLD: night-violet ground (#120e1c), plum surfaces (#171229), hairline violet rules, one bright violet accent (#9d7cff) on near-black text; Impact condensed caps for standings, quiet Trebuchet body, mono for rating readouts.

STORY: A visitor reads "this store keeps a real ladder", sees every game ranked with its live offer count, and understands the whole funnel — pick a division, configure, get promoted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: masthead mono strip, big caps "CLIMB THE RANK. SKIP THE GRIND.", a real standings panel (POS · cover · GAME · entry track · entries) with the leader row highlighted and the violet rule animating in, primary action "View the ladder" inside the panel; subhead and count strip below.

FORM: Ranked Ladder standings sheet, #4 on my ordered list, seed a5134147 (safer register, user's pick).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
